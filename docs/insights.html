Channel Insights

What works on The Dungeon Dive, and what to try next ·
Breakout ratioA video's views divided by the median of the ~20 uploads published around it. 1.0× is a typical upload. Neighbours are the same age, so old videos get no head start.
Evergreen rateViews per day still being earned by videos more than a year old, measured over the last 90 days. This is what the back catalogue earns from search and recommendations.
Public data onlyViews, likes, comments, lengths, titles and transcripts. There is no access to impressions, click-through or retention, so these are inferences. Each card shows its sample size and how sure the inference is.

Recommendations

Generated from the data below on every refresh. Each card shows its evidence, a confidence grade (based on sample size and a bootstrap interval that must clear 1.0×), example videos, and an experiment to try.
