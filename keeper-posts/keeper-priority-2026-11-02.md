# A Revised Order of Descent

A revised chart of the lower passages arrived this cycle, bearing ten approved routes and several vigorously erased annotations. The map-room clerk has requested that all objections be submitted with evidence of an actual expedition within the past two years. This has considerably thinned the queue at his desk, though the surviving petitioners have brought rather large boxes.

**Newly shelved:**

- [Top 10 Dungeon Crawls of All Time (2026 Edition)](https://dungeondive.quest/t/2343) — fresh contenders, familiar corridors, and the practical matter of fitting an expedition on the table.

**From the deeper stacks**, earlier charts remain available for those tracing the movements of the favourites:

- [The Top 10 Dungeon Crawls of all Time (2024 edition)](https://dungeondive.quest/t/15) — the previous survey, with Silver Tower still among the leading lights.
- [Top 10 Dungeon Crawls - Part One](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1397) — the earlier descent begins with Space Hulk and ends amid Brimstone's sprawling supplies.
- [Top 10 Dungeon Crawls - Part Two](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1396) — Hellboy, two Warhammer Quests, and the enduring hazards of the Lost Tomb.

Planned excavations resume next cycle.
