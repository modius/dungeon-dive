# More Potions for the Strongroom

Four crates arrived this cycle, two glittering so fiercely that the receiving clerk had to borrow the astronomer's spectacles. Inside were additional heroes, malicious wizards, a dragon, and sufficient potions to keep the infirmary supplied through another regrettable expedition. The quartermaster has asked whether any of the bottles might instead contain a more interesting artifact. He has been directed to the fan-made annex, where such requests tend to become entire systems.

**Newly shelved:**

- [HeroQuest + AxianQuest Infinite Dungeons = the way it should be played (HeroQuest Solo)](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1427) — scripted quests give way to procedurally generated passages and uncertain encounters.
- [HeroQuest - Episode 3 - Customizing HeroQuest for Solo Play Without an App](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1432) — card supplements and house rules assemble an analogue replacement for Zargon.
- [Three new expansions for HeroQuest! (Against the Ogre Horde, and others)](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1431) — earlier reinforcements enter the dungeon for an AxianQuest trial.
