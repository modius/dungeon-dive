# Thirteen Without an Auction

A merchant's circular arrived this cycle bearing the extraordinary claim that its treasures could be obtained without first defeating a collector at auction. The acquisitions clerk has pinned it above his desk, between the unpaid invoices and a drawing of a cupboard he hopes someday to own. Its assurances are dated and geographically qualified; he has underlined both provisions, having once paid more for a parcel's passage than for its contents.

**Newly shelved:**

- [13 Great Games You Can Buy Right Now!*](https://dungeondive.quest/t/2354) — thirteen adventures Daniel found at official US stores on November 14, with the asterisk duly examined.

**From the deeper stacks**, two of the circular's exhibits already have substantial dossiers:

- [The Restless - The Masterwork of an Outsider (Review)](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1403) — four unlikely travellers cross a disintegrating continent toward its tainted gods.
- [Horrified - A Perfect Game (for what it is)](https://dungeondive.quest/t/2165) — familiar monsters, distinct little puzzles, and a welcoming first expedition for unsuspecting friends.

Planned excavations resume next cycle.
