# Fresh Despatch

A slim envelope this cycle — the Keeper weighed it, frowned, and concluded there was hardly anything inside at all. Which, it turned out, was precisely the point.

- [When Less is More - Minimalism in Table Top Adventure Games](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1949) — three ways to do more with less: no art, no flavour text, one action a turn.

**From the deeper stacks** — if a small box tempts you, the archive keeps its earlier miniatures:

- [Search for the Emperor's Treasure](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1821) — Tom Wham's doodle-simple classic, the very game that inspired this despatch.
- [Dragons Down - An Adventure for the Ages](https://dungeondive.quest/t/308) — not a single sentence of flavour text; every story is one you write yourself.
- [Small Box Thunderdome!](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1681) — four crawls the size of a card wallet, the Adventure of D among them.

Planned excavations resume next cycle.
