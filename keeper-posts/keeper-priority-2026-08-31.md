A parcel this cycle, and heavier than it needed to be. There were standing instructions at the door that no further boxes of this particular kind be accepted — and the archivist signed for one himself, at the counter, on the grounds that it was bought at retail and therefore did not count.

- [Cthulhu Death May Die: 2026 Revisit and a Look at Season 4 (review)](https://dungeondive.quest/t/2104) — a pyramid, three pieces of a staff, and investigators who grow deadlier the nearer they come to dying.

**From the deeper stacks** — the same table, earlier verdicts:

- [Cthulhu: Death May Die - Review (unboxing and reboxing)](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1548) — the Wild Bunch against the Elder Gods, and an insert that lets you shut the box for a whole game.
- [Returning to Cthulhu: Death May Die - An Insanely Good Game](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1549) — Hastur loose in a casino, and a Great Old One who arrives with no lore attached to him.
- [How I deal with FOMO, and why I am not backing the new Cthulhu Death May Die campaign](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1552) — four years ago he asked for small-box expansions instead; this week he asks again.
