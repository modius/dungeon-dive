# Fresh Despatch

One parcel this cycle, and it arrived by way of surrender. The archivist had spent a week with a rulebook that would not yield — read three times, understood not at all — and rather than press on he reached down the shelf for something he knew by heart. The Keeper approves entirely: a rulebook that defeats a careful reader has failed at its one duty, and the shelf exists so the defeated have somewhere to go.

What he reached for is nearly gone: one used copy at one dealer, the expansions vanished. The Keeper has watched many games pass from print into rumour, and notes that this one is only paper and a few dice — the easiest sort of thing to bring back.

**Newly arrived:**

- [Hero's Journey Home: Charming and Fun](https://dungeondive.quest/t/2202) — you have already slain the demon king; the game is the walk home.

**From the deeper stacks** — the same shelf, further back:

- [Revisiting the Hero's Journey Home - solo card-based adventure game](https://dungeondive.quest/t/2073) — the game that shipped with no box at all, in a burlap sack.
- [Pauper's Ladder - a perfect level of abundance (a comprehensive overview)](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1807) — the charm and the artwork this one is measured against.
- [Revisiting Iron Helm: This or that and press your luck](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1680) — the press-your-luck that this journey deck deliberately does without.

Planned excavations resume next cycle.
