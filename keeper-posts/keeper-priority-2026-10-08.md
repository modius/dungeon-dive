# A Book About Everything Else

A slim parcel arrived from Brighthelm this cycle, addressed to whoever keeps the books. That is me, and I resent how pleased it made me. Inside: a field guide that changes not one rule, yet somehow the island is larger than it was yesterday.

**Newly shelved:**

- [Pauper's Ladder - The Brighthelm Chronicle Expansion (Review)](https://dungeondive.quest/t/2304) — an expansion with no new rules, only lore, art and checkboxes for nearly every card.

**From the deeper stacks**, for those who want the island before its encyclopaedia, or the guide that came first:

- [Pauper's Ladder - The King Crimson and Genesis of Adventure Games](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1806) — Talisman and Prophecy, as a young Peter Gabriel might have designed them.
- [A Look at Moon Towers, an Expansion for Paupers' Ladder](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1808) — four hundred cards, fourteen birds, and a field guide you can colour in.
- [Pauper's Ladder - a perfect level of abundance (a comprehensive overview)](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1807) — the whole ecosystem walked in one sitting, the Dungeon Dive included as a destination.

Planned excavations resume next cycle.
