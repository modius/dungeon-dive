# A League of Questionable Associates

A charter arrived this cycle authorising the formation of a new league within the Archive. Its members include four mercenaries, a necromancer of disputed credentials, and several cardboard persons whose references proved surprisingly sound. The recruiting clerk has accepted them all, provided they bring their own bases. He is less pleased by the charter's provision for unexpected complications, having spent years arranging that nothing unexpected should happen before luncheon.
