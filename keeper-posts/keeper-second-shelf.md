# The Second Shelf

Every keeper knows a collection is two things wearing one coat. There are the boxes — the dice, the tiles, the miniatures that clatter — and behind them, always, the books that first taught the boxes how to dream. This cycle the Keeper pressed a hidden panel at the back of the game-wing and stepped through into the second shelf: the reading room's older, dustier cousin, where the archivist put down the cardboard entirely and simply walked the reader along his own bookcases, one shelf at a time.

The spine of it is *Shelf by Shelf* — five unhurried tours through a library kept in no order but affection's, from a wall of vintage Philip K. Dick out through Lansdale, Sturgeon, Ballard, Blatty and the reclusive Ligotti. Around that spine the wider library gathers its stranger holdings: weird westerns summoned by a Shadows of Brimstone habit, a Fighting Fantasy history that finally names its artists, two gorgeous Games Workshop tomes, a stack of isometric vampire zines, and a frozen brain waking a thousand years too late. It opens on a literary weather report and closes, years on, with a cyberpunk novel nobody ever remembers to recommend.

No dice are thrown in this wing. But the Keeper has long suspected it holds the truer collection — the one the games were only footnotes to.

**Exhibit Catalogue** — the second shelf, earliest first:

- [Beyond the Reading Rainbow - Literary Update](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1971) — Fracassi's flat-liner, Ted Chiang in person, and a Gotrek omnibus cracked open.
- [Shelf by Shelf - Part One](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1974) — the tour begins: a wall of vintage Dick, Dunsany from Tolkien's own shelf.
- [When the West Gets Weird](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1964) — a Brimstone habit down the weird-western trail; the Dark Tower, read seven times.
- [Choose Your Own Adventure - House of Danger](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1763) — a Choose Your Own Adventure classic dragged, dice and all, onto the table.
- [Shelf by Shelf - Part Two](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1963) — Lansdale where others draw the line; Simak's pastoral stars; Wolfe by lucky accident.
- [Shelf by Shelf - Part Three](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1972) — Michael Cisco's buried genius, Sturgeon's next question, Otomo's untouchable *Akira*.
- [Win a copy of Michael Cisco's Unlanguage](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1970) — a giveaway, long closed, for a book he confesses he isn't sure he likes.
- [Shelf by Shelf - Part Four](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1965) — Ballard splits a reading life in two; Blatty's faith; the Ligotti story that scared him.
- [The House on Abigail Lane - Horror Novella Round-Up](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1907) — a haunted house documented in scholarly footnotes, narrated over live thunder.
- [YOU Are the Hero - Damn Right I Am!](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1969) — the Fighting Fantasy history that credits every artist, and a spine read to bits.
- [The World of Warhammer: Official Illustrated Guide](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1968) — a gorgeous Old World encyclopedia that, being *illustrated*, credits not one artist.
- [Super Blood Harvest (Omnibus)](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1973) — three isometric vampire-space zines, rules-light enough to actually run.
- [Shelf by Shelf - Part Five](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1967) — Stapledon's *Sirius* wept over on a bus, Silver John's Appalachia, Rucker's transrealism.
- [Solis, by A. A. Attanasio](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1966) — a frozen brain wakes as a mining machine: Attanasio's overlooked 90s cyberpunk.
- [...and the Gunslinger Followed - We Deal in Lead](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1915) — the library's obsessions loop back to the felt: a lead-slinging solo western.
