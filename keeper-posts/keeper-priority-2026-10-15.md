# The Costliest Crate

A crate arrived from the far east of the auction-houses this cycle, packed in old newsprint, and the Archive's treasurer has not yet stopped weeping. Inside is a dungeon of four painted floors, a castle with a closing door, and a spinner. The crate's sender would like it known that all games with spinners are great.

**Newly shelved:**

- [Keeper of the Hidden Treasure: Zombie Game - A Look at the Rarest Dungeon Crawl I Own](https://dungeondive.quest/t/2327) — glorious 1984 art, a humble game, and zombies you set on your friends.

**From the deeper stacks**, for those who want the crawls it is measured against:

- [TSR's The Classic Dungeon - RetroLook](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1857) — the plain, roomy board that Daniel ranks above this one.
- [Dark World (1992) - RetroLook](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1859) — another case of a magnificent toy and a weak game.
- [DungeonQuest (FFG and GW) - Top 10 Reevaluation](https://dungeondive.quest/t/605) — the brutal benchmark every spin-and-pray crawl answers to.

Planned excavations resume next cycle.
