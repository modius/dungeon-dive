# One Parcel, Reluctantly Shelved

The courier left a single crate this cycle and would not meet my eye about it. Inside, a game the archivist wanted very much to love and could not — filed here anyway, because a catalogue that only records enthusiasm is not a catalogue.

**Newly shelved:**

- [The Witcher: Old World - I should love this game (but I don't!)](https://dungeondive.quest/t/2237) — every ingredient correct, and no dish; a monster hunt with nothing to fail on the road to it.

**From the deeper stacks**, since the shelf above is an argument rather than a verdict:

- [The Witcher Adventure Game - These House Rules Make it Great!](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1172) — the older Witcher the archivist still prefers, rescued by the best house rules he has met.
- [Talisman 5e - Home Brewing Solo/Co-op Rules (A Work in Progress)](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1169) — what he does when a game withholds tension: builds the missing rule himself, on a 2d6 chart.
- [Relic - is it just Talisman in space?](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1252) — sixty per cent familiar, and the other forty is why it works.

Planned excavations resume next cycle.
