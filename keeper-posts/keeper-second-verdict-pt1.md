# The Second Verdict

There is a door at the back of this archive that the Keeper does not advertise. Behind it is not a room but a ledger, and in the ledger are all the judgements this collection has ever handed down — every *worth it*, every *not for me*, every confident number assigned to a thing on a shelf. It is a large ledger. It is written in pencil, which visitors sometimes find alarming.

They shouldn't. Pencil is the honest medium. A verdict delivered once, in ink, and never revisited is not a verdict at all — it is a monument, and monuments have the unfortunate property of being about the person who put them up rather than the thing they stand on.

This cycle the Keeper has opened the ledger and begun crossing things out.

What emerges is a small and slightly embarrassing pattern. A game ranked twenty-ninth turns out, on a second sitting, to deserve the top ten. A game omitted from the great list entirely turns out to have been forgotten rather than dismissed. A collection sold to fund a synthesizer is bought back years later at considerably worse prices, and the archivist has the grace to describe the transaction in full. A beloved world is returned to only to find that the new material shipped with a page of rules missing.

None of this is failure. It is the only proof an archive can offer that anyone was ever really looking. The first verdict tells you what a thing seemed to be. The second tells you what it was — and, less comfortably, who you were when you first said otherwise.

Eleven amendments are on display below, arranged as they happened. Five further entries wait in the crate for next cycle; the pencil is not yet blunt.

**Exhibit Catalogue** — second thoughts, earliest first:

- [Fortune & Glory - Revisiting a Game I Should Have Ranked Higher](https://dungeondive.quest/t/2087) — he telephones the President to airstrike some Nazis, then re-rates the game twenty places.
- [The Return of the Walking Dead - Revisiting Here's Negan](https://dungeondive.quest/t/2086) — an escort mission where the man you're escorting won't open doors, on principle.

- [Top Five Games I Sold and Bought Again](https://dungeondive.quest/t/2089) — Space Hulk, Kingdom Death and Arkham Horror, each sold once and repurchased at leisure.
