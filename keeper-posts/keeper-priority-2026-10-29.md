# Lanterns Out

A parcel arrived at the Archive's back door this cycle, and the courier did not stay for a receipt. Inside were pamphlets that smelt of damp basements and a single cassette that nobody has yet dared to play. The candles in the reading room have been guttering ever since.

**Newly shelved:**

- [Spooky Solo RPGs to Play in the Dark](https://dungeondive.quest/t/2340) — a Halloween table of solo horror, from cursed VHS to a 90s serial-killer hunt.

**From the deeper stacks**, for those who prefer their terrors already catalogued:

- [A Look at Alone Against Fear](https://dungeondive.quest/t/2161) — one survivor, seven Hell Gates, and no phone signal anywhere in town.
- [A Guide to Solo Analog Horror (Solo RPG)](https://dungeondive.quest/t/31) — eleven games of static, occultists and unseen worlds.
- [Ghost Transmission - Cassettes are Back! In Haunted Form! (analog horror)](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1227) — a journaling game played through the hiss between stations.

Planned excavations resume next cycle.
