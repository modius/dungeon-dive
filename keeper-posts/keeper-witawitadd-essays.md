# What I Talk About When I Talk About Dungeon Diving

There is a wing of the archive with nothing in it.

No boxes, no tiles, no rank of painted miniatures behind glass. A chair. A camera. Shelves left deliberately out of focus. The Keeper avoided the room for years on the reasonable grounds that an archive of games ought to contain games, and this one contains only a man explaining himself.

That was an error of cataloguing.

*What I Talk About When I Talk About Dungeon Diving* ran from 2019 to 2022 — nine essays in which the archivist set the cardboard down and worked backwards from the shelf to the reason for the shelf. Why nostalgia is maintenance rather than regression. Why an empty room is an event. Why he would rather roll badly than think poorly. When the series retired it returned as *Diving In*: same habit, better lighting.

The wing never stayed put; its arguments kept escaping into the review halls. So the catalogue below hangs as one continuous run: the essays, and the evidence they left behind.
