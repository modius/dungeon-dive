# The Hobbycast — The 2022 Run

There is a door in the east wall that the Keeper has been meaning to catalogue for some time. It is not a display room. There is nothing behind it to look at — no boxes, no tiles, no glass. Only a chair, a microphone, and the accumulated evidence of a man talking for a year about everything he was not supposed to talk about.

The reason for the room is stated plainly in the first minute of the first recording. The archivist had worked out that the machine which distributes his voice rewards narrowness and punishes range; that mentioning a novel in a video about cardboard measurably costs him. So rather than stop mentioning novels, he built a second room off the side of the main hall and put in it every subject the machine would have penalised. Science fiction paperbacks, read aloud off a physical stack with the back-cover copy still attached. Japanese animation. Radio dramas from 1955. Other people's very small channels. Two dozen games sold in a fortnight, each with a cause of death.

What the Keeper did not expect, cataloguing it, was how much of the room turns out to be confession.

This is the wing where the archivist admits that three of his favourite video games simply outran his reflexes and he will not be finishing them. Where he admits that playing a board game is sometimes the least enjoyable part of owning one — and then builds a rather good argument for why that is the medium's fault and not his. Where he counts up the collectible cards he gave away in 1999 and arrives at a figure he would rather not have arrived at.

It bookends itself neatly. Episode 2 sets out ten qualities he wants in a dungeon crawl. Episode 26 sets out ten faults he cannot forgive — and illustrates every single one with a game he loves. Between the two sits an ask-me-anything of such length and candour that it functions as an autobiography with dice in it.

The catalogue below runs in the order the episodes were recorded. Some were hung today. Some have been on the wall for seasons. The Keeper declines to say which.
