# The Spring 2019 Field Season

Every archive has a season it keeps circling back to. For this shelf it is the spring of 2019, when the Keeper's correspondent went to ground for four months and sent back a run of field reports on the miniatures-adventure games then crowding his table — the sort with a squad of heroes, a fistful of dice, and monsters that simply will not stop arriving.

What makes the season worth reshelving as a set is the argument it conducts almost by accident, spread across four boxes. At one end stands the ascetic: a Weird War skirmish so lean its whole rulebook runs ten pages and its heroes are nothing but a hand of cards, spent down to nothing. At the other, a Hellboy box so swollen with Kickstarter abundance that its retail twin looks half-starved beside it. Between them: an Iron Kingdoms swamp that plays like chess against bog-trogs, and a beast epic of mice and cheese that smuggles *Watership Down* onto the dungeon floor.

Two threads run on well past that spring, and the catalogue follows them where they lead — the Hewitt-and-Williams design bloodline that quietly binds Hellboy to Silver Tower, and Hellboy's own long road back to the table, seven years later.

**Exhibit Catalogue**

- [Fireteam Zero - A Weird War 2 game - Review](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1950) — five minis, ten pages, and thirty-minute defeats you somehow don't resent.
- [Widower's Wood - An Iron Kingdoms Adventure Board Game - Review](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1952) — two-square movement turns a swamp into a chessboard of bog-trogs.
- [Hellboy: The Board Game: The Review: The Video](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1951) — a game he adores and can't quite recommend; blame the retail box.
- [Mice and Mystics - an overview / review](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1953) — cheese for currency, dice as drama, and a genuine beast epic underneath.
- [Warhammer Quest: Silver Tower - Part One](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1510) — the Hewitt-Williams masterclass whose design DNA runs straight through Hellboy.
- [Hellboy: The Board Game - 2026 Revisit](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1921) — seven years on, the same box returns to argue for the top ten.
