# Further Down the Rock

A crate came up from the mines this cycle, still warm, still humming, and smelling faintly of beer. The dwarves have sent back their expansions, and they would like it noted that the beer is part of the rules.

**Newly shelved:**

- [Deep Rock Galactic - A Look at the Expansions](https://dungeondive.quest/t/2322) — a four-mission campaign, a pub between shifts, and a game allergic to reference cards.

**From the deeper stacks**, for those who want the first descent, or the tunnels that led there:

- [Deep Rock Galactic - Deluxe 2nd Edition (Review)](https://dungeondive.quest/t/2229) — dull to set up, then played three times in one day.
- [Dwarves delving for gold and glory! A review and overview of Stalagbite!](https://dungeondive.quest/t/2122) — the goblin hunting party is coming, so spend your glory to linger.
- [Zombicide Invader - Review](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1888) — the farewell to a franchise that Deep Rock would later replace.

Planned excavations resume next cycle.
