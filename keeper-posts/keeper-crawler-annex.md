# The Crawler Annex — Corridors Left Unmapped

Every archive has a wing the surveyors skipped. Ours sits behind the dungeon-crawl stacks, through a door everyone assumed was a cupboard. This cycle the Keeper finally took a lantern and a ball of string and went in. The string ran out. The corridors did not.

This is the Crawler Annex: ten dungeon crawls that slipped past every previous excavation, now mapped and shelved beside a few old acquaintances. There are graveyards and sewers in it, a cultist fortress, a forest with a frog in it, and a realm of death with its own goddess. There's also a dungeon that would very much like the heroes to go away. Some of these came in on a Kickstarter pledge and some were bought off eBay with the previous owner's notes still in the margins. One came in a box held together with tape and good intentions.

As a set, the wing is mostly about the gap between a game's box and the game inside it. A cheap-looking crawl turns out to have teeth. A magnificently produced one ships with a rulebook that recommends you read a different rulebook. A game the Keeper's patron can't bring himself to like is the one he'd hand to any newcomer. Small boxes get praised for holding big games, and the same box is later begged not to grow. Two crawls appear twice, once at the unboxing and once on the far side of actually playing, so a reader can watch first impressions meet the dice.

The catalogue below runs in order of excavation, oldest first. Bring string, and more of it than the Keeper did.
