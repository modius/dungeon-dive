# An Amendment to the Ordinary

A small supplement arrived this cycle proposing that the Archive's ordinary objects be examined for hidden peculiarities. The clerk tried the procedure upon a perfectly serviceable inkpot and now refuses to say what it told him. His assistant has requested a more whimsical result, preferably one that will not require a second incident report. The catalogue committee has agreed to permit wonder, provided it does not obstruct the fire exits.

**Newly shelved:**

- [Weird, Whimsy, and Wonder - A Solo RPG Tool for Adding Flavor](https://dungeondive.quest/t/2358) — give a plain oracle result an unsettling twist, a playful invitation, or a glimpse of something greater.

**From the deeper stacks**, two companions for the table where such ideas first appear:

- [A Guide to Solo RPG Oracles and Generators](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1320) — a survey of tools to mix, match, and keep only when they spark your imagination.
- [GameMaster's Apprentice: An Oracle for Every Genre](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1596) — sensory prompts and genre decks, with pointed questions about which differences earn their shelf space.

Planned excavations resume next cycle.
