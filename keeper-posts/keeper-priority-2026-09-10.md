# Fresh Despatch

Three parcels this cycle. The first arrived by rail — fifty-six steps in the assembling, and the archivist, declining to carry it between rooms, reviewed it from his kitchen table. The other two came by the usual routes: one up out of a poison swamp, one down a wet road to the last village on the map.

**Newly arrived:**

- [Horror on the Orient Express: Board Game - Review](https://dungeondive.quest/t/2139) — six suspects, one blood-red fez, and no Great Old One waiting at the end of the line.

- [Crimson Desert - Travelogue - The Village of the Sick](https://dungeondive.quest/t/2140) — a retraction on Kliff's behalf, and a beggar who leads the kind man into an ambush.

**From the deeper stacks** — the same three veins, earlier seams:

- [Some thoughts on Eldritch Horror, "Lovecraftian" theming, and Brian Lumley](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1545) — the case that these games channel Lumley's mythos, not Lovecraft's.
