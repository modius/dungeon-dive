# Ends of the World — Wastelands, Weird Wars, and Doomed Skirmishes

A crate arrived this cycle with its address scorched off and a Geiger counter taped to the lid. The courier said it came from the end of the world. Pressed for detail, he said it came from several, which the Keeper found less reassuring.

This is Ends of the World, a short wing for the games that start after things have already gone wrong. Here the bomb has fallen, the dead have stood up, or the war that was supposed to end everything has simply kept going. Some exhibits put you behind the wheel of something armoured and on fire. Some lock you in a ruined city with not enough ammunition. One sits you in a radio booth and asks you to keep broadcasting. Another sends you to hunt the monsters the apocalypse left behind. And one, against every instinct of the wing, hands you a battlefield and asks you to stop the fighting rather than win it.

What links them, the Keeper thinks, is that every one of them knows the ending already. They're more interested in the people still walking around after it, and in what they're carrying, who they're driving with, and how long the fuel holds. Several are skirmish games that fit on a small table. Daniel spends a fair amount of this wing arguing that you don't need a basement of terrain to play them.

Older dispatches from the same wastes have been salvaged and shelved among the new arrivals, from the Road Warrior monologue that opened 2021 to a zombie game finally played the way it always wanted to be. Walk the wing in order and the ends of the world line up, more or less, like mile markers.
