# The Quick Delve — DungeonQuest & the One-Evening Dungeon

The delivery this cycle was small enough to arrive under one wing: eight panels, a purple whisky bag the archivist insists is the game's most important rule, and a label in his hand reading *one evening, no more*. The Keeper, which begins its working day at the hour these games insist you leave, has never understood the hurry. It has filed them anyway.

The wing is built around a single castle. Dragonfire Castle has been entered in this archive in 1987 clothing and in 2014 clothing, by a painted barbarian, by a wizard with a strength of three, by a hero called Chelara who reached the exit and met a spear. Three of the eight new panels go back in, and three older panels hung alongside never came out. The archivist quotes the same figure each time, across five years and two editions: fifteen per cent survive. He says it the way other men say grace.

What the Keeper noticed, shelving, is that none of these games is about the treasure. Every one of them is about the door. DungeonQuest is won by whoever leaves. Dungeon Run hands the winner a stone and sets five players on their heels to the exit. One rulebook in this wing names *escape the dungeon* as its tutorial quest and forgets to say how; another describes line of sight so drily that the archivist preferred the instructions for his kitchen tap. A labyrinth on a three-by-three grid scrolls off the table behind you like an old console screen, taking the monsters and, if you were careless, half a medallion. The long crawls in this archive are about the descent. The short ones are about the way out before the sun reaches the skull.

The Keeper records, without comment, that a fan-made merchant in this wing now sells an item called the Keeper's Kiss: two thousand four hundred gold, every attribute raised, and wounds to anyone standing beside you. The Keeper did not license the name. It considers the pricing fair.

Three older panels hang alongside, undated. The Keeper has stopped marking which are new, on the grounds that the castle has not changed either.
