# Loose Pages

Not every gap in an archive announces itself. The obvious absences — the missing volume, the empty plinth — are found by anyone with eyes. The dangerous ones are the exhibits that *look* finished: a case sealed, a label written, a card beside the door reading COMPLETE, and behind the glass a story stopping one page short of where it meant to.

The Keeper spent this cycle walking the older wings with a lamp and a grudge, and came back with loose pages.

There is no theme here beyond that — five recoveries with nothing in common except the holes they fill. A plague arrives in a kingdom of criminals a year after the archive last looked in on it. A Russian novel from 1972 turns out to have been quietly authoring half the apocalypses in the collection, and gets its own board at last. A game explains itself in seven pages and is filed beside the ones that take seventy. And two Mork Borg wings — one of skirmish mats, one of black water — receive the panels they were always short of.

Filed together, they read less like a batch than like a repair. The exhibits below are shelved as one continuous run: some panels hung today, some hung seasons ago, and the Keeper declines to say which is which. That is rather the point of a repair.
