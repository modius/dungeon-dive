# Fresh Despatch

A single parcel this cycle, its wrapping stitched from broad leaves and smelling of wet stone and old jungle. The Keeper turned it over once — an island, apparently, that remembers being robbed — and shelved it before the ancestors inside grew restless.

- [Dungeons of Galora - A Solo, dungeon crawl, RPG](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1976) — Latin American folklore, ten dungeons, and a fight that asks only that you outlast the round.

**From the deeper stacks** — if that lean little crawl tempts you, the archive keeps its kin:

- [Fixing Tedious Combat in Solo RPGs](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1902) — the very sermon Galora practises: keep the fight short, let the dice decide fast.
- [Corrupted Crypts - Solo Dungeon Crawl](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1887) — another one-author crawl that wrings a great deal out of very little.
- [2D6 Dungeon](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1703) — the last time the archivist was genuinely excited about combat in a crawl.

Planned excavations resume next cycle.
