# Fresh Despatch

Two deliveries this cycle, arriving down opposite corridors and declining, on inspection, to be shelved together. One is a man forty hours into a poisonous swamp on principle, allowing himself a single attempt at the bottom because the climb back up is not to be spoken of. The other is a box lifted off an ordinary shelf at an ordinary price, with no campaign to pledge and nothing whatsoever to wait for — a transaction the Keeper notes has become rare enough to warrant the ledger.

**Newly arrived:**

- [A Normal Guy Plays Dark Souls - Part 6 - Exploring the Swamp and Getting a Huge Sword](https://dungeondive.quest/t/2112) — one descent, no resting halfway, and a greatsword he is nowhere near strong enough to lift.
- [The Dwarves: Big Box - Review](https://dungeondive.quest/t/2113) — a fast, tense little game slowed down by meeples the archivist would rather were cubes.

**From the deeper stacks** — earlier entries in the same two veins:

- [Rune - Yes Indeed. Prepare, Engraved, to roll dice (Dark Souls on your table).](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1832) — the solo Souls-like judged truer to the tone than the licensed board game ever managed.
- [Ten Tabletop Games for fans of Dark Souls](https://dungeondive.quest/t/2033) — among them a guidebook to a video game that does not exist.
- [Defenders of the Realm - a long and casual discussion](https://dungeondive.quest/t/2095) — the thematic *Pandemic* this week's dwarves are plainly descended from.

Planned excavations resume next cycle.
