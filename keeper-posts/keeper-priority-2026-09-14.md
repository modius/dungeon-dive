# Fresh Despatch

One parcel this cycle, and it arrived by hearsay — a reader's comment beneath the Dark Souls list, which the archivist followed to a search box and from there to a purchase. Inside: a dying sun, a city coming apart beneath it, and a box that looks as though it ought to need forty pages of rules and instead carries two poker cards. The Keeper, who shelves games by their rulebooks and has filed a great many whose rulebooks outweighed their games, does not yet know where to put this one.

**Newly arrived:**

- [Sunrot - The Simplest Game I've ever Played](https://dungeondive.quest/t/2175) — roll a four, flip a card, and discover the wound you dealt was your own.

**From the deeper stacks** — grime, restraint, and the list that started it:

- [An overview of Forbidden Psalm - Skirmish gaming in the world of Mork Borg](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1259) — the grimdark skirmish game this box's terrain would furnish, played here with Pathfinder pawns.
- [When Less is More - Minimalism in Table Top Adventure Games](https://dungeondive.quest/t/1949) — take one piece away from Glory and it stops being a game.
- [Ten Tabletop Games for fans of Dark Souls](https://dungeondive.quest/t/2033) — the list beneath which the comment was left; five criteria set out before a single game is named.

Planned excavations resume next cycle.
